Slide

A calming scroll.

Feel the pull to open a social app? Open Slide instead.

Scroll as long as you like through slow, soothing backgrounds. There is no feed, no notifications and nothing to catch up on. Your thumb gets its scroll, your mind gets a break.

Slide is free, open source and has no ads.

Tip: add Slide to your home screen, right where your usual app used to be.
